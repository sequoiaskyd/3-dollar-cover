The Note

Who We Are

$3 Cover is a two-guitar duo built on a simple idea: a great song is worth re-opening, not just repeating.

We pull from five decades of popular music, the 70s to right now, and take it apart. A ballad becomes a slow burn. A radio hit gets stripped to its bones and rebuilt with two guitars and a little bad attitude. You'll know the song. You won't know what we did to it.

We don't play songs. We re-open old wounds.

Between us there's a couple of decades of playing, a shared refusal to phone it in, and zero interest in the note-for-note tribute circuit. We're faithful to nothing and true to ourselves, and that's exactly what makes it worth the cover charge.
